Colored lines show median fitted responses with shaded pointwise 2.5th–97.5th percentile ranges across usable simulated studies. Click a design's legend entry to hide or show its curve and shaded region together. These ranges describe variation across simulated studies under the source and measurement assumptions; they are not confidence intervals within one fit or simultaneous whole-curve bands. A median needs one usable value; a range needs at least two. Failures remain in the displayed fit counts.

The gray dashed curve is the central assumed generating reference when supplied. Identical reconstructions for a fixed environment are shown once, without an across-study range: its original observations are held fixed in every simulated study. This does not establish that the underlying curve is known. Fitted responses use the analyzed scale and may extrapolate outside a design's sampled times; inspect the candidate sampling times when interpreting recovery.
