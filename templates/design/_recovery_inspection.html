Numerical recovery values and usable study counts are available below.

Colored lines show median recovery across successful simulated studies. Optional shaded ranges run from the pointwise 2.5th to 97.5th percentiles. Their spread reflects the sampled reference trajectories and future measurement assumptions. This variation across successful studies differs from uncertainty within a single fit or in the reference alone. The pointwise ranges are not simultaneous bands. A range requires at least two usable values at that time; a median can use one. Fit failures and usable counts remain visible.

Recovered response is compared with the central reference, shown as a gray dashed line. Recovery error means recovered minus that simulated study's own true response, calculated before summarizing; its plot has a zero-error guide. All values use the analyzed response scale. Recovery outside a schedule's sampling range depends on model extrapolation; see its timeline for sampling coverage.
