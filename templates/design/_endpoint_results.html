Recovered times use equally weighted simulated studies with valid endpoint outcomes. Failed fits and invalid curves are excluded; valid curves that do not reach the threshold retain their mass beyond the assessment horizon. The central 95% range is the empirical 2.5th–97.5th percentile range across studies, not a confidence interval for one fit. At least two valid outcomes are needed for a range. Counts use all simulated studies as their denominator.

Timing errors compare each recovered endpoint with that study's own true endpoint, using only pairs where both times are finite. Read errors with the finite-pair count and crossing outcomes: two noncrossings do not imply zero error. Crossings are linearly interpolated on the assessment grid including the exact baseline; nothing is extended beyond the assessment horizon. A dash means unavailable. All times and timing errors use in vivo time units. These endpoints assess the suggested schedules and do not change the optimization criterion.
